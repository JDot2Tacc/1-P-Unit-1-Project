The Shows biggest Moments

  • A-Train Obliterates Robin (Season 1): In the series premiere, A-Train runs straight through Hughie’s girlfriend Robin at super-speed, leaving Hughie holding just her hands and setting off the entire plot.
  • Homelander on Flight 37 (Season 1): Homelander abandons the hijacked plane full of passengers, telling a terrified child and dying senator that saving them would expose his location, proving his complete lack of empathy.
  • Herogasm (Season 3): The infamous superhero-only annual private adult retreat delivers a wildly unhinged episode filled with shocking powers and chaotic character interactions.
  • Stormfront vs. The Girls (Season 2): Maeve, Starlight, and Kimiko team up for a brutal, cathartic beatdown of the Nazi supe Stormfront.
  • Butcher Tears Victoria Neuman in Half (Season 4): In a shocking display of newfound powers, Butcher brutally rips Victoria Neuman apart.
